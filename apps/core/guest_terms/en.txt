## 7. House rules

7.1 No smoking: smoking of all kinds is prohibited inside the unit, including cigarettes, cigars, shisha, electronic cigarettes and vaping devices. The ban includes bedrooms, bathrooms and the kitchen. Opening a window or using an extractor does not permit smoking. Observe disclosed building restrictions; permission to smoke on balconies or at entrances must not be assumed without explicit notice.

7.2 No parties or disturbance: parties, disruptive gatherings, loud music and noise that harms neighbours or building users are prohibited at all times.

7.3 Occupancy: only the approved people and number may stay, without exceeding capacity. Pets are prohibited in all apartments, including Marrakech. Unit-specific visitor rules and the counting of children and infants are disclosed before booking.

7.4 Safety and lawful use: do not tamper with smoke detectors, safety equipment or locks, or use the unit unlawfully. Report any incident or hazard immediately.

7.5 Property and facilities: use appliances and furniture with care and do not remove unit belongings. Respect shared areas and residents' privacy, keep entrances and emergency exits clear, and use only authorised parking.

7.6 The booking holder must inform companions of these rules. Responsibility for their actions is determined by the facts and applicable law, not absolute responsibility for an act that cannot legally be attributed to the holder.
